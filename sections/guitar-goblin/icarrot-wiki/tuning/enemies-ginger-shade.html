    styles/enemies/ginger_shade.tres

    Numbers for the Ginger Shade (scripts/ginger_shade.gd): The Ginger's immune spectral projection that haunts whichever boss room is fighting. Every shade shares one of these (styles/enemies/ginger_shade.tres); edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Players have 100 health.

    Described on: Ginger Shade

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/ginger_shade_stats.gd.
