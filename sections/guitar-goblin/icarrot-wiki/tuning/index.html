    styles/ammo_drops.tres25Destiny-style ammo bricks: every enemy that dies may drop a special (green) or heavy (purple) brick, at most one per kill. The host rolls each drop and tells every machine; each player gets their own copy that only they can pick up, so partners never fight over ammo. Lives in styles/ammo_drops.tres; every number is a slider.Ammo
    styles/burrow_keys.tres8The colours of an encounter's keys: each boss room's torches, the buff its boss drops and the hub pedestal it belongs on all share one colour, so players can tell which goes where.Burrow Encounter
