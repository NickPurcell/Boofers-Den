    styles/enemies/ginger.tres

    Gameplay numbers for The Ginger, the burrow encounter's main boss (scripts/ginger.gd): its health and how the pedestal orbs unlock it, the float, the taunting immune phase, the transformation, the four attacks (purple bombs, magic bolts, floor eruptions, the root sweep), the shield, the enrage and the juice. Every ginger shares one of these (styles/enemies/ginger.tres); edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Players have 100 health; enemy HP is in revolver units (a body shot does 10, a headshot 20).

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/ginger_stats.gd.
