    styles/gear_tuning.tres

    Feel knobs for the carrots' hero gear and face: the floating boots' walk cycle and footsteps, how the cape, epaulette tassels and belt strap swing, and where the face and gear sit on the body. Shared by every player via styles/gear_tuning.tres. Heights are in pixels up from the carrot's tip (the body is 36 tall; the leaves start at about 34).

    Described on: Appearance

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/gear_tuning.gd.
