    styles/grenade.tres

    The energy ball every carrot carries (G or right mouse): a crackling plasma orb that's lobbed, bounces, and bursts in an electric shockwave. How it's thrown, how it flies, how big the burst is, how fast it recharges and its colours. Lives in styles/grenade.tres; every number is a slider.

    Described on: Energy ball

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/grenade.gd.
