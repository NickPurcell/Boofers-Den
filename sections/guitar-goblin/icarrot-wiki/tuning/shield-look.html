    styles/shield_look.tres

    How a carrot's shield breaking, staying down and recharging looks and sounds (the numbers it runs on are in styles/tuning.tres: Shield and health). Lives in styles/shield_look.tres.

    Described on: Health

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/shield_look.gd.
