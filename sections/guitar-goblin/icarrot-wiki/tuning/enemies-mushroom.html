    styles/enemies/mushroom.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the mushroom (the portobello gunslinger). Every mushroom shares styles/enemies/mushroom.tres; edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Its acceleration and jump height are in its movement tuning (styles/mushroom_tuning.tres).

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/mushroom_stats.gd.
