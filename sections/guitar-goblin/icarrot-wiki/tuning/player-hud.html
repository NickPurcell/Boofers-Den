    styles/player_hud.tres

    How your own shield and health show: the bars at the top of the screen and the red "jelly" that creeps in from the screen's edges as your health runs low. Lives in styles/player_hud.tres. (The numbers they show are in styles/tuning.tres: Shield and health.)

    Described on: Health

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/player_hud.gd.
