    styles/enemies/sweet_potato.tres

    Gameplay and feel numbers for the Sweet Potato boss (scripts/sweet_potato.gd). The default instance is styles/enemies/sweet_potato.tres; tweak it in the Inspector (or live on the boss in the Remote tree). Enemy HP is in revolver units: a body shot does 10, a headshot 20. A carrot runs at 115 px/s.

    Described on: Sweet Potato

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/sweet_potato_stats.gd.
