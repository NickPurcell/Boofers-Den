    styles/enemies/asparagus.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the asparagus (the perched sniper that spits its scales). Every asparagus shares styles/enemies/asparagus.tres; edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree.

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/asparagus_stats.gd.
