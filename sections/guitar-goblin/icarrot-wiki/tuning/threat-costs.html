    styles/threat_costs.tres

    How much each enemy kind adds to a fight: its threat cost. A Squad's threat is the sum over its members, and each BossRoom shows its beats' threat so the fight's difficulty curve can be read without playing. One shared table (styles/threat_costs.tres); bosses aren't counted.

    Described on: Fight Pacing

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/encounter/threat_table.gd.

    ParameterValueRangeWhat it does
    costsDictionary[String, float]({ "asparagus": 1.5, "corn": 5.0, "mushroom": 2.0, "pepper": 3.0, "potato": 3.5, "runner": 0.6, "pumpkin": 1.5, "zombie": 1.0 }) script default {}Enemy kind (a key of Level.KINDS) -> its threat: roughly the damage it threatens per second, times how long it lives under fire, times how hard it is to ignore. A kind missing here counts as 1. The reasoning behind the current numbers (zombie = 1): - zombie: slow melee that rarely reaches a moving player; dead in under a second. - pumpkin: dies fast, but sprints at you and its blast must be killed or dodged first. - asparagus: fragile, but perched out of reach chipping away; someone has to go and hunt it. - mushroom: the most damage of the small adds, and it backs off, so it takes chasing. - pepper: lobs that deny ground plus a breath, and it lasts twice as long as a zombie. - potato: a tank whose charge forces a dodge every few seconds; it splits the squad's attention. - corn: a mini-boss with volleys of kernels; a squad on its own.
