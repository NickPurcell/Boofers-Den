    styles/enemies/pepper.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the hot pepper brute (scripts/pepper.gd): health, the measured advance, the lobbed fireball and the point-blank flame breath. Every pepper shares one of these (styles/enemies/pepper.tres); edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Players have 100 health; enemy HP is in revolver units (a body shot does 10).

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/pepper_stats.gd.
