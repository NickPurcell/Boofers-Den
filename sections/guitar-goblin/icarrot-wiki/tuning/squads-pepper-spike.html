    styles/squads/pepper_spike.tres

    A hand-picked group of enemies a fight's Beat brings in together: which kinds, how many of each, and how they arrive. Squads are small reusable files (styles/squads/*.tres), so the same squad can open one fight and pad another. Its threat (ThreatTable) is what the BossRoom readout shows.

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/encounter/squad.gd.
