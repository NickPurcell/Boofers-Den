    styles/hud.tres

    How the bottom-left weapon HUD looks: the grenade recharge icon, the ammo pips and the gun's name. Lives in styles/hud.tres.

    Described on: Weapon HUD

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/hud_style.gd.

    ParameterValueRangeWhat it does
    Layout
    scale21 to 4Pixel scale of the whole HUD (1 = one game pixel per art pixel).
    margin(10, 10)Distance from the bottom-left corner of the screen, in game pixels.
    gap5 px0 to 20Space between the grenade icon, the ammo and the name (in HUD pixels).
    max_bullet_pips121 to 40Guns with up to this many rounds show one bullet per round; bigger magazines show thin ticks.

    bullet#f2c450
    bullet_tip#fff0a8
    hull#e8483a
    hull_base#f2c450
    spent#3a3450A spent round's slot.
    empty_warn#ff5a4aThe slots pulse this colour when the gun is empty.
    name_color#ffe2b0
    name_top#ffffff
    reload_alpha0.550 to 1The name and pips dim to this alpha while reloading.
    Energy ball
    orb_radius5 px2 to 12Radius of the energy-ball icon.
    grenade_dim0.30 to 1Brightness of the part of the icon that hasn't recharged yet.
    fill_edge#f4fdffThe rising line at the top of the fill.
    fill_glow0.60.2 to 4How bright the icon is while it fills, and once it's ready. The icon's white core is 1.4x this, so about 0.7 keeps it from blooming at all.
