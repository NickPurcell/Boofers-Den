    styles/enemies/runner.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the Rot Runner (the starved, skinny rotten carrot that sprints at players).

    Described on: Rot Runner

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/runner_stats.gd.
