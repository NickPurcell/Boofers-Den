    styles/enemies/pumpkin.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the pumpkin (the jack-o'-lantern that runs at players and blows up).

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/pumpkin_stats.gd.

    ParameterValueRangeWhat it does
    Health
    hp30 hp1 to 2000Hit points. A revolver body shot does 10.
    shot_knock30 px/s0 to 200How hard a bullet shoves it sideways.
    Movement
    speed98 px/s0 to 300Sprinting speed (a carrot runs at 115).
    creep14 px/s0 to 100Creeping speed toward the target while the fuse burns.
    lunge150 px/s0 to 500Horizontal speed of a lunge across a gap or onto a block.
    jump_cooldown0.6 s0 to 4Seconds before it can jump or lunge again.
    stuck_dist3 px0 to 20Moving less than this over stuck_time while trying to run means something's in the way: hop.
    stuck_time1.2 s0.1 to 8How long it runs without getting anywhere before it hops.
    Fuse
    arm_dist30 px0 to 150Lights its fuse when a player is this close (vertical distance counts a bit less).
    arm_spread6 px0 to 40Extra lighting distance per pumpkin (0, 1 or 2 times this), so a pack doesn't all go off at once.
    fuse_time1.05 s0.1 to 5Seconds from lighting the fuse to the boom.
    chain_fuse0.3 s0 to 2Fuse for a pumpkin lit by another pumpkin's blast (chain reactions).

    blast_radius46 px0 to 200Blast radius, measured to the nearest point of a carrot or enemy.
    blast_knock320 px/s0 to 1000How fast the blast flings carrots away.

    blast_damage45 hp0 to 200Damage to each player caught in the blast (players have 100 health).
