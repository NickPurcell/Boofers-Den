    styles/enemies/red_corn.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the Big Red-and-Black Corn boss (scripts/red_corn.gd): health, its heavy stumbling gait, the husk telegraph, the twin kernel streams and fans, the popcorn mortars and their burning patches, the husk slam and the enrage. One shared resource (styles/enemies/red_corn.tres); edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Players have 100 health; enemy HP is in revolver units (a body shot does 10, a headshot 20).

    Described on: Red Corn

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/red_corn_stats.gd.
