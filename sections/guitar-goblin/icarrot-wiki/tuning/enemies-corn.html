    styles/enemies/corn.tres

    Gameplay numbers for the Corn Ogre (scripts/corn.gd): health, its lumbering, stumbling gait, the husk-opening telegraph and the kernel volley. Every corn shares one of these (styles/enemies/corn.tres); edit it in the Inspector, or live via the Remote tree. Players have 100 health; enemy HP is in revolver units (a body shot does 10).

    Described on: Big Cobber

    Open this file in the Godot Inspector to change these values. While the game runs from the editor, most of them can also be changed live through the Remote scene tree.

    Its fields are defined in scripts/enemy_stats/corn_stats.gd.
