    A tall asparagus sniper that drops onto a high perch, puffs up its cheeks behind a laser sight and spits a glowing scale at you.

    Behaviour

    Spear-Gus never walks. It falls out of the sky (with a whistle) under 900 px/s² of gravity and stakes itself into a plank perch with a thud, a wobble and a spray of wood chips. From then on it stays put. In the Arena it lands on the perches near the inner ends of the side planks, high enough to snipe across the screen; if one is taken it uses the other side, then spots further out along the planks.

    Once planted it watches the nearest player, turning its aim toward them at 4 rad/s, and leans toward them a little. Bullets don't shove it around.

    Attacks

    Spat scale

    It can only start a shot at a player within 420 px of its mouth with a clear line: walls and planks both block its view. Its first shot can't come sooner than 1.1 s after it plants itself.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Breath in 0.9 s minus the lock A rising inhale. Its cheeks swell and a thin pink laser runs from its pursed lips to you, getting brighter. The aim still follows you, but slower (2 rad/s).
    Lock 0.3 s The laser turns bright and blinks fast. The aim is frozen. Move now.
    Ptoo! Instant The cheeks empty and a glowing scale flutters out along the laser's last line.

    The scale flies straight at 260 px/s and vanishes after 2 s if it hits nothing. Walls and planks stop it. A hit deals 20 hp (players have 100 hp) and knocks the carrot 110 px/s along the scale's flight and 70 px/s upward. A scale that strikes a carrot's head does 2x times the damage. Spear-Gus aims a little above your feet, at 17 px, so standing tall in its path is the worst place to be.

    After a shot it waits between 1.5 s and 2.3 s before the next wind-up.

    Strategy

    • Watch the laser. As long as it is faint and following you, you're fine; the moment it locks and blinks, step, jump or dash off the line. The shot goes where the laser was, not where you are.
    • Dash i-frames beat every attack in the game: dashing through a scale lets it fly on past.
    • Break line of sight. It won't wind up without a clear view, and planks block both its view and its scales. The patch of ground right under its own plank is safe.
    • It never moves and isn't knocked back, so it is an easy target for steady fire, and headshots on its tall bud are easy to land.

    Headshots

    Its head box is the top of the spear: the purple bud and the face, down to just below the mouth. Headshots there do the weapon's headshot multiplier (see the weapon pages, such as Shotgun).

    Co-op

    The host runs Spear-Gus's aim and timing. The start of a wind-up and the shot are announced to every machine, so everyone hears the inhale, sees the laser, and sees the scale leave from the same spot at the same angle. Every machine flies the scales itself and checks only its own carrot against them, so a hit always matches what that player saw. It targets the nearest player, so whoever is closest should be the one watching the laser. Dash i-frames dodge a scale for everyone.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one Spear-Gus is in the standing roster, replaced by another whenever it dies.
    • Burrow Encounter: in the Red Corn room, two of them perch on the high ledges at either end of the room as adds, and come back a while after each death until the boss is down.

    Design notes

    The wind-up is long and the aim keeps following until the lock, so a player can't dodge early and must time it. The short lock at the end is the fair part: the laser shows exactly where the shot will go and leaves just enough time to get off the line. Spear-Gus never moves, so it is a target to prioritise rather than something that chases you.
