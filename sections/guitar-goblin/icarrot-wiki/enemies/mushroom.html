    Behaviour

    Shroom is a gunslinger: a big brown cap, a bandana over its face and two pistols. It goes after the nearest player but doesn't want to get close. Each Shroom picks its own preferred distance, somewhere between 95 px and 140 px, and is happy anywhere within 24 px of it.

    • Too far: it walks in at 42 px/s.
    • Too close: it backs away at 32 px/s, still facing you, but never off the end of its plank or into a wall.
    • Right in its face (closer than 30 px): it hops backwards, 110 px/s back and 230 px/s up.

    Each Shroom walks at its own pace, its speeds scaled by a random multiple up to 0.15x above or below 1, and two Shrooms shuffle apart rather than stand inside each other.

    When it can't see you, it closes in. If you're on another surface, it first looks for a spot on its own surface with a clear shot; failing that, it follows the level's route map like the Zombie: up through planks, lunging across gaps at 140 px/s, and down through planks. It waits 0.8 s between jumps, and hops if it shuffles for 1.5 s without getting anywhere.

    Attacks

    Pistol burst

    It only starts a burst standing on the ground, with a clear line to you, within 230 px. Its first burst comes between 0.6 s and 1.6 s after it arrives.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Aim 0.45 s to 0.65 s A cock-click, both pistols raised, and a glint on the barrel. The aim tracks you quickly (6 rad/s). This is the telegraph.
    Burst 3 shots, 0.2 s apart Alternating pistols, each shot wobbling up to 0.07 rad either way. The aim now turns slowly (0.9 rad/s), so you can run out from under it.
    Pause 1 s to 1.8 s It repositions before the next aim.
    Reload 1.6 s, once every 2 bursts It twirls its pistols. It still moves, but can't shoot. A short wait of 0.2 s to 0.5 s follows.

    If it loses sight of you while aiming, it gives up and waits 0.4 s before trying again. Shooting it while it aims stretches the aim out a little.

    Its slugs are slow glowing magenta bolts: 170 px/s, slow enough to jump over. They fly straight, stop on walls and planks, and fade after 2.5 s. Each one deals 15 hp (players have 130 pts shield and 70 hp health) and knocks the carrot 110 px/s along its flight and 90 px/s upward. It aims 17 px above your feet, and a slug that lands on a carrot's head does 2x times the damage.

    Strategy

    • The click and the glint are the cue. Jump over the slugs, or move sideways once the burst starts: the burst turns slowly, so running outpaces it.
    • Dash i-frames beat every attack in the game: a dash through a burst lets the slugs pass.
    • Count bursts. Once every 2 bursts it reloads; that is the time to push in.
    • Rushing it works. Up close it backpedals or hops away instead of shooting, and it can't back off the end of a plank.
    • Break line of sight to stop a burst before it starts.

    Headshots

    Its head box is the cap, which is wider than its body. Headshots there do the weapon's headshot multiplier (see the weapon pages, such as Shotgun). The wide cap makes headshots easy to land.

    Co-op

    The host runs Shroom's AI and physics; everyone else follows its streamed state, so all players hear the same cock-click and see the same reload. Every shot is announced to every machine, which flies the slug itself and checks only its own carrot against it, so a hit always matches what that player saw. It targets the nearest player; a second player off to the side can shoot it freely while it duels the first. Dash i-frames dodge the slugs for everyone.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one Shroom is in the standing roster, replaced by another whenever it dies.
    • Burrow Encounter: an add in two boss rooms, coming back a while after each death until the boss is down:
      • the Red Corn room, where it bursts up out of the floor;
      • the Ghost Pepper room, where it starts high on the right.

    Design notes

    Shroom is the ranged enemy that moves. The range band keeps it out of slap range and in the open, and the slow slugs and slow-turning burst make it a test of footwork rather than reflexes. The reload gives a clear window to rush it.
