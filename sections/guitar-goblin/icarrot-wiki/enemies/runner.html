    The Rot Runner is filler: cheap, plentiful and fast. It looks like a Zombie that hasn't eaten in weeks: a big gaunt head with sunken, glinting eye sockets and a hungry gaping jaw, a stringy ribbed body, wilted leaf hair and long spindly root arms that flail behind it as it sprints. It comes in numbers, it dies in a couple of revolver shots, and its job is to keep players moving and shooting.

    Behaviour

    It sprints at the nearest standing player at about 106 px/s, nearly as fast as a running carrot, so you can only open distance with a sprint or a dash. Each runner's top speed varies by up to 0.08 either way, so a pack strings out instead of arriving as one block.

    Like the zombie, it follows the level's route map to reach you on another surface: it jumps up through planks from below, lunges across gaps at 160 px/s, hops onto solid blocks and drops through planks to get down. After a jump or lunge it waits 0.45 s before another. If it runs for 0.9 s without getting anywhere, it hops to free itself.

    In a pack, each runner aims at a spot up to 14 px to one side of you while it's still far off, stops at its own distance (8 px plus up to 10 px), and eases off when another runner is right ahead of it, so a pack fans out around you rather than stacking on one pixel.

    If every carrot is knocked out, it runs over to them but doesn't attack.

    Attacks

    Claw swipe

    When a standing player is within 26 px sideways and 22 px vertically, it stops and goes for you.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Wind-up 0.3 s It shrieks, rears back with its claws up behind its head and trembles, glaring. This is the telegraph.
    Strike 0.1 s It throws itself forward at 140 px/s with its claws raking out 26 px in front and its jaws snapping. The claws hurt only now.
    Recover 0.28 s It stays bent over, panting.

    A swipe deals 10 hp (players have 130 pts shield and 70 hp health) and knocks the carrot back a little. It can start another swipe 0.5 s after the last one ends.

    Taking hits

    Every hit staggers it for 0.14 s, stopping it in its tracks, and shoves it back. It flashes white at most once every 0.25 s, so a pack under SMG fire doesn't strobe. When it dies it shoots up stiff, then folds into a heap and blinks out over 0.45 s in a spray of goo and leaf bits.

    Strategy

    • One alone is easy: a couple of revolver body shots, or a single headshot. The danger is several arriving from both sides while you're busy with something bigger.
    • Keep moving. You can outrun a runner with a sprint or a dash, then turn and shoot.
    • The wind-up is short but clear: when the claws go up, dash through it or jump. Dash i-frames dodge the swipe.
    • Its stagger means steady fire holds the front runner of a pack back.

    Co-op

    The host runs the runner's AI and physics; everyone else follows its streamed state. When a swipe starts, the host announces it and every machine plays the same timeline. Each machine checks its own carrot against the claws, once per swipe, so a hit always matches what that player saw.

    Design notes

    The runner is the pressure enemy. The Zombie is slow and tanky and the pumpkin punishes standing still with a blast; the runner simply makes standing still uncomfortable, a lot of the time, for little damage each. It's priced low on the threat scale so fights can bring them in numbers.
