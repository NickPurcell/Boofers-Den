    Behaviour

    The zombie goes after the nearest standing player. On the same surface it simply walks at them with a lurching gait, top speed 14 px/s, dragging a foot between steps. Otherwise it follows the level's route map: it jumps up through planks from below, lunges across gaps at 140 px/s, and drops through planks to get down. After a jump or lunge it waits 0.8 s before another.

    If it shuffles for 2 s without getting anywhere, it hops to free itself. In a crowd, each zombie stops a little further back than the last, so they bunch up around a player instead of stacking on one spot.

    Attacks

    Slap

    When a standing player is within 36 px sideways and 24 px vertically, the zombie plants its feet and slaps.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Wind-up 0.5 s It rears back with its arm raised. This is the telegraph.
    Swing 0.1 s The hand sweeps out 34 px in front of it. The hand hurts only now.
    Recover 0.45 s It stays put in the follow-through.

    A slap deals 15 hp (players have 100) and kicks the carrot away and upward. It can start another slap 0.9 s after the last one ends.

    Strategy

    • Dashing through the swing dodges it: dash i-frames beat every attack in the game.
    • The wind-up is long. Back off or jump when the arm goes up.
    • Each hit shoves a zombie back a little, so steady fire holds one at arm's length.

    Co-op

    The host runs the zombie's AI and physics; everyone else follows its streamed state. When a slap starts, the host announces it and every machine plays the same timeline. Each machine checks its own carrot against the hand, once per slap, so a hit always matches what that player saw.
