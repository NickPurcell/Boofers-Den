    Behaviour

    The Potato Bull is a big lumpy potato on four stubby legs, with gnarly purple sprouts curling off its eyes like horns and bristling down its back, and a grumpy bull muzzle with flaring nostrils.

    It stays on the ground and does not use the level's route map. It trots after the nearest player at up to 30 px/s, following them left and right, and hops up low steps in its way. Turning round is slow: it plants its feet and swings about over 0.35 s, so getting behind it buys time. It stops trotting 34 px from its target and waits for a chance to charge. If a player gets closer than 26 px, it shuffles back a step to make room for a run.

    Bullets barely nudge it (6 px/s per hit), and only while it trots.

    Attacks

    Charge

    It charges when a player is in front of it, no more than 330 px away and at least 26 px, roughly level with it (feet no more than 30 px apart vertically), with no wall in between. Its first charge comes 1.2 s after it appears; after that it waits 1 s to 2 s between charges.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Wind-up 1 s It stops, faces you, paws the ground so dust kicks back, lowers its head so the sprout horns point forward, snorts steam, and its eyes go red, with a rumble. This is the telegraph.
    Charge Until it passes you, or 2.2 s It barrels straight ahead, up to 270 px/s, with a dust trail, speed lines and screen-shaking footfalls. It can't steer. If you run on ahead of it, it keeps going after you, and it brakes only once it is 70 px past you.
    Skid Until it stops It brakes hard in a spray of dust.
    Recover 0.7 s It shakes itself off and turns to face its target, then trots again.

    Running into a player during the charge deals 35 hp (players have 130 pts shield and 70 hp health) and launches them along the charge and upward. It hits each player at most once per charge.

    Wall slam

    If it runs into a wall while charging or skidding at 110 px/s or more, it slams: the screen shakes, it bounces off, and it is stunned for 2.2 s, wobbling under dizzy stars. Then it recovers as above. Slower than that, it just stops.

    Headshots

    The weak spot is the brow and horn roots on the front-top of its head. While it is stunned, the weak spot grows down over most of its body (2.6x its usual height), so almost every shot counts as a headshot.

    Strategy

    • Stand with your back to a wall. When it winds up, step aside or jump over it, and it will slam the wall behind you and stun itself.
    • During the stun, empty your gun into it. This is the punish window, and almost everything is a headshot.
    • Dashing through the charge dodges it: dash i-frames beat every attack in the game.
    • It only charges at players roughly level with it. Standing on a plank above it is safe from the charge.
    • Its turn is slow. Jumping over it mid-charge leaves it facing the wrong way for a while.

    Co-op

    The host runs the Potato Bull's AI and physics and streams its state, charge direction and charge number to everyone else. Each machine checks its own carrot against the charging potato, once per charge, so a hit always matches what that player saw. The wall slam is an event every machine plays.

    Death

    It bursts into fries: a flash, a splat of mash, fries and chunks of potato raining down, its sprouts flying off, a cloud of starch and a big thump.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one potato is in the arena's enemy roster, so one Potato Bull is in every wave and comes back after it dies.
    • It does not appear as an add in the Burrow Encounter. Its huge cousin the Sweet Potato is the boss of the encounter's top room.

    Design notes

    The bull is a lesson in using the level: the charge is dangerous in the open, but a wall turns it into a free damage window. The Sweet Potato builds its whole fight on the same idea.
