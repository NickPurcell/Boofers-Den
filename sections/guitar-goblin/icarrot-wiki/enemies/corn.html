    Behaviour

    Big Cobber is the biggest thing in the Arena: a towering ear of corn on stubby legs, wrapped in a green husk jacket, with a silk-tassel mop, a heavy brow and an underbite of kernel teeth. It is slow and heavy, and it shrugs off a lot of lead.

    It lumbers toward the nearest standing player one step at a time. Every footfall thuds, puffs dust and nudges the screen. A step only moves it for part of the stride and peaks at 34 px/s, so on average it creeps along far slower than a carrot runs. Now and then a step turns into a stumble: it lurches forward faster and further, lands with a bigger thud, and teeters for 0.8 s before it catches itself.

    It stops about 60 px short of its target (a little further for each extra corn, by 14 px, so two of them don't stand on the same spot). It never jumps onto planks; it only hops heavily up ledges no taller than 26 px. If a player stands on a plank right above it, where it has no line of fire, it backs away for 1.5 s to get an angle. If it walks for 2.5 s without getting anywhere, it hops to free itself.

    Bullets barely shove it (8 px/s per hit).

    Attacks

    Kernel volley

    When a player is within 400 px and it has a clear line from its belly to them, it stops between steps and attacks. It first waits 1.6 s to 2.6 s after it appears, and 2.4 s to 3.6 s between volleys.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Open 1.2 s It pulls its husk open like a jacket (the peel takes 0.4 s). The belly kernels glow hotter and hotter while it turns its aim toward you. For the last 0.3 s the aim stops following you. This is the telegraph.
    Fire One kernel every 0.12 s A stream of glowing kernels sprays from the belly (count: 9x), sweeping across 0.55 rad through the aim. The aim still drifts after you, but slowly (0.45 rad/s).
    Close 0.6 s The husk folds shut and it walks on.

    Each kernel flies at 230 px/s for up to 2.5 s, deals 8 hp (players have 130 pts shield and 70 hp health) and knocks the carrot along the shot and upward. A kernel that strikes a carrot's head does 2x times the damage. Kernels that hit a wall or the floor pop into popcorn.

    It can aim up to 1.15 rad above straight ahead and 0.7 rad below, so a player high above it or tucked right under its belly is out of its arc.

    Headshots

    While the husk is closed, the weak spot is the face and tassel at the very top of the cob. While the husk is open (during the telegraph and the volley), the weak spot moves to the glowing belly kernels in the middle of its body. Shots there show kernels flying; shots on the husk tear off green shreds instead.

    Strategy

    • The open husk is both the warning and the opening. Put headshots into the glowing chest during the telegraph, then get out of the line before the aim locks.
    • The volley sweeps one way across a fixed arc. Move against the sweep, or jump over the low end of it. Once the aim locks it barely tracks you, so sideways movement beats it.
    • Dashing through the stream dodges it: dash i-frames beat every attack in the game.
    • It needs a clear line from its belly. A plank between you and it stops the volley entirely, though it will back off to find an angle.
    • It is slow. Kiting it is easy; the danger is the volley landing while you are busy with other enemies.

    Co-op

    The host runs Big Cobber's AI and physics and streams its state (husk opening, aim, gait, stumble) to everyone else. Every kernel is announced as an event, and each machine launches the same kernel and checks only its own carrot against it, so a hit always matches what that player saw. Kernels in flight keep going even if the corn dies first.

    Death

    It bursts in a shower of popcorn: a flash, kernels popping one after another, husk leaves fluttering down, silk strands drifting and a big thump.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one corn is in the arena's enemy roster, so one Big Cobber is in every wave and comes back after it dies.
    • It does not appear as an add in the Burrow Encounter. Its big, angry cousin Red Corn is the boss of the encounter's middle room.

    Design notes

    Big Cobber is built like an Ogre from a shooter raid: a slow, tanky body with a big, readable charge-up whose weak spot is exposed only while it is about to fire. The open husk turns the telegraph into a risk-reward choice: stay in its line for the crit, or get clear.
