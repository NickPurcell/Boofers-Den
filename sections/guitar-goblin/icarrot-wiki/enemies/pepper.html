    Behaviour

    Hot Pepper is the big brute: a red chili with a green stem cap, angry eyebrows and smoke curling off its head. It is tough, with 80 hp. Which move it uses depends on how far away the nearest player is: fireballs from afar, flame breath up close.

    It advances in a measured way: it walks at 22 px/s for 0.7 s to 1.4 s, then pauses for 0.35 s to 0.8 s, and repeats, throwing fireballs in between. Once a player is within 95 px it stops stepping and hustles in at 40 px/s for a breath. After a throw, if you're within 140 px, there's a chance (back_chance) it backs off a little for 0.35 s to 0.6 s.

    To reach you on another surface it follows the level's route map like the Zombie, at 1.4x its walking speed: up through planks, lunging across gaps at 140 px/s, and down through planks. It waits 0.8 s between jumps and hops if it walks for 1.5 s without getting anywhere. Bullets shove it 35 px/s each.

    Attacks

    Lobbed fireball

    It throws when you are between 75 px and 330 px away sideways and it has a clear arc to you. Its first throw comes 0.6 s to 1.4 s after it arrives.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Wind-up 0.65 s It rears back with a fireball held over its head. This is the telegraph.
    Throw Instant The fireball leaves in a high arc with an ember trail.
    Recover 0.35 s It stays put in the follow-through.

    The arc is solved to land on you. It aims ahead of a moving player by 0.6x their speed times the flight time (at most 60 px) and misses by up to 12 px either way at random. The fireball falls under 520 px/s² and fizzles after 3 s.

    • Direct hit: 20 hp, with a knock of 250 px/s along the throw and 250 px/s upward. A direct hit on a carrot's head does 2x times the damage.
    • Landing: it bursts into flame where it hits the floor or a wall. A carrot within 13 px takes 20 hp and is knocked away at 210 px/s.

    Sometimes (double_chance) it throws a second fireball straight after the first, with a shorter wind-up of 0.38 s. Otherwise it waits 1.5 s to 2.6 s before the next throw. If a plank overhead blocks every arc, it hustles toward you for 0.6 s and tries again 0.3 s later.

    Flame breath

    When you are within 46 px sideways and 26 px vertically, it breathes fire.

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Puff up 0.55 s Its cheeks swell and glow. It turns toward you at twice its breath turn speed. This is the telegraph.
    Breath 0.9 s A roar and a cone of fire 66 px long, 0.34 rad either side of its aim. It creeps forward at 10 px/s and the flames follow you at 1.2 rad/s, tilting up or down by at most 0.4 rad.
    Recover 0.5 s It stands still.

    A carrot in the flames takes 15 hp every 0.3 s and is pushed away from the mouth (210 px/s sideways, 160 px/s up). A carrot can't be hurt again during its short grace after a hit, so in practice the flames burn about once a second. Walls and planks shield you from the cone. The next breath can't come for 1.4 s to 2.2 s, and it won't throw again for 0.6 s after a breath.

    Strategy

    • At range, keep moving. The throw leads you, so change direction once the fireball leaves its hand; standing still only leaves the random miss to save you. Watch where it lands as well: the splash hurts too.
    • Up close, back off or jump over it when it puffs up. The cone is short and only tilts so far, so getting above it or behind it is safe.
    • Planks protect you from both attacks: a plank overhead blocks its arcs, and the flames stop at walls and planks.
    • Dash i-frames beat every attack in the game: dash through a fireball or the flames.
    • It is slow. With 80 hp it takes a while to bring down, so fight it from mid-range between throws.

    Headshots

    Its head box is the top of its body, from the stem cap down through the angry face. Headshots there do the weapon's headshot multiplier (see the weapon pages, such as Shotgun). It is big, so its head is an easy target.

    Co-op

    The host runs Hot Pepper's AI and physics, and streams its state and aim to everyone for the poses. Each throw and the start and end of each breath are announced to every machine. Every machine flies the fireballs itself, draws the flames, and checks only its own carrot against the fireball, the splash and the cone, so a hit always matches what that player saw. It goes after the nearest player, so the player furthest away is safest to shoot it. Dash i-frames dodge everything it does.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one Hot Pepper is in the standing roster, replaced by another whenever it dies.
    • It isn't an add in any Burrow Encounter room yet.

    Design notes

    Hot Pepper covers every range so you can't simply stand back from it: the fireballs punish standing still at range, and the breath punishes standing next to it. The stop-and-go advance and the long wind-ups keep that fair, and its slow, measured walk is what makes it read as a heavy brute.
