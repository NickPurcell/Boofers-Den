    A jack-o'-lantern on stubby vine legs that sprints at the nearest player, lights its fuse and blows itself up.

    Behaviour

    Jacko is a kamikaze. It sprints at the nearest standing player at 98 px/s, a little slower than a running carrot, so you can outrun one on open ground but not for long in a cramped spot. Like the Zombie, it follows the level's route map to reach you on another surface: it jumps up through planks from below, lunges across gaps at 150 px/s, hops onto solid blocks and drops through planks to get down. After a jump or lunge it waits 0.6 s before another. If it runs for 1.2 s without getting anywhere, it hops to free itself.

    Once it is on the ground within 30 px of its target (vertical distance counts a bit less), it lights its fuse. Each pumpkin adds 0, 1 or 2 times 6 px to that distance, so a pack running in together doesn't all go off at the same moment.

    Attacks

    Self-destruct

    Phase Lasts What you see
    Light Instant It skids to a stop with its legs braced, sparks spit from its stem and the fuse starts fizzing.
    Fuse 1.05 s It creeps toward you at 14 px/s while it swells, shakes, and its face glows hotter and whiter. It blinks slowly at first, then faster and faster, with a beep on each blink.
    Boom Instant Fire, rind, seeds and smoke, a shockwave ring and a big screen shake.

    The blast reaches 46 px, measured to the nearest edge of each carrot's body, so clipping the edge of it counts. Each carrot caught takes 45 hp (players have 100 hp) and is flung away from the centre at 320 px/s, always with an upward kick. Blasts aren't shots, so there is no headshot bonus on them.

    Friendly fire and chain reactions

    The blast hurts other enemies too. Every non-pumpkin enemy in range takes 30 hp. Other pumpkins in range don't take damage; they get lit instead, on a short 0.3 s fuse (a pumpkin already lit has its fuse cut to that, if it was longer). One boom in a pack can set off the whole pack in a quick ripple.

    Popping versus exploding

    How a Jacko dies depends on whether its fuse is lit:

    • Shot dead before the fuse is lit: it pops in a harmless squelch of pulp and seeds.
    • Shot dead after the fuse is lit: it explodes on the spot, at full strength.

    Strategy

    • Kill it early. With 30 hp it pops quickly, and a popped Jacko does nothing.
    • Once it is lit, stop shooting it unless it is near enemies and far from you. Killing a lit Jacko only makes the boom come sooner.
    • The fuse is your dodge window. Back out past 46 px while it creeps at 14 px/s, or dash through the boom: dash i-frames beat every attack in the game.
    • Use it. Lead a Jacko into a crowd, let it light, and get clear: the blast deals 30 hp to everything else around it and lights any other pumpkins.
    • Each hit shoves it back 30 px/s, which buys a little room while you finish it.

    Headshots

    Its head box is the stem and the top of its face: the upper part of the pumpkin. Headshots there do the weapon's headshot multiplier (see the weapon pages, such as Shotgun).

    Co-op

    The host runs Jacko's AI and physics; everyone else follows its streamed state. Lighting the fuse and the boom are both announced to every machine, so everyone sees the same fuse and the blast in the same spot. Each machine then checks only its own carrot against the blast, so a hit always matches what that player saw. The host alone applies the blast to other enemies and lights other pumpkins. Dash i-frames dodge the blast like any other attack.

    Appearances

    • Arena: one Jacko is in the standing roster, replaced by another whenever it dies.
    • Burrow Encounter: in the Ghost Pepper room, one Jacko comes in high on the left as an add and comes back a while after each death until the boss is down.

    Design notes

    The fuse is long on purpose. A kamikaze that went off on contact would be a coin flip; with the skid, swelling and speeding-up blinks, a player who is paying attention always has time to back off or dash. The pop-versus-boom rule rewards killing it early, and friendly fire plus chain reactions turn it from a pure threat into a tool.
