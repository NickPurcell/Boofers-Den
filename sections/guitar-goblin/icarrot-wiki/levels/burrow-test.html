    The Burrow Test was the first level bigger than the screen. It's a cosy underground warren several screens wide and tall, built to prove out the Terraria-style camera and the Warm Burrow art. Each of the other biomes has a test level built the same way.

    Route

    1. Entrance shelf: you drop in at the top left on a broad dirt shelf, under a low dirt ceiling. A couple of root ledges hang overhead.
    2. The descent: two big dirt steps lead down and to the right, toward the bottom of the level.
    3. Arena chamber: a one-screen room at the bottom with root ledges at two heights and a giant glowing mushroom in the middle. Enemies walk in from both sides here.
    4. The climb: to the right of the arena, branch ledges and two tall mushrooms zig-zag up to a high exit pillar at the top right.

    Camera

    Outside the arena, the camera follows your own carrot. It glides after you at 7 /s, aiming a little above your feet (Vector2(0, -24)), and never shows past the level's edges. Fall fast enough and it leads below you, so you can see where you'll land.

    When you step into the arena chamber, a CameraZone takes over and the camera eases into a fixed shot of the whole room at 6. Leave it, and the camera goes back to following you. In co-op, every machine frames its own carrot, so players can be in different shots.

    What it's made of

    • Ground: Solid blocks with the Warm Burrow look: chocolate soil with orange taproots and worms.
    • Ledges: Planks with the Warm Burrow look: branch roots with dangling hairs.
    • Mushrooms: Shroom platforms with amber caps that glow and bloom.
    • Backdrop: three parallax layers of roots and burrows, each scrolling slower the further away it is, plus blurred root silhouettes passing in front of the camera.

    See Level Parts for how each piece is edited.

    Design notes

    • Blocks are kept few and large on purpose. Each block paints its own edges, so blocks that touch show a seam.
    • Some glowing domes in the far backdrop can read as platforms. Backdrops should never look walkable.

    Tunable parameters

    These are read straight from the game's files, so they are always the current values. Open the file in the Godot Inspector to change them.
