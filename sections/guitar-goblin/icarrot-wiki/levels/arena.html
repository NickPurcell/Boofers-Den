    The Arena is where the game began: a single screen of grassy hillside under a blue sky, with drifting clouds, distant hills and three wooden planks to fight from. Every enemy kind shows up here, so it's the test bed for new enemies and weapons.

    Layout

    • Ground: a strip of grass across the bottom. The grass and dirt are painted into the baked backdrop, with an invisible collision block under them.
    • Planks: three one-way wooden planks. Two sit low on the left and right, and a wider one sits high in the middle. You can jump up through a plank from below, land on top, and press S to drop through. Enemies route over them too.
    • Walls: invisible walls at both edges keep everyone on screen.
    • Spawns: players drop in from the sky. Enemies walk in from both edges, and perching enemies like the Asparagus land on the outer planks.

    Enemies

    Enemies arrive one after another from alternating sides. Each one is replaced by the same kind a short while after it dies, so the fight never lets up. The roster runs from zombies and pumpkins up to the Corn ogre. --enemies= on the command line picks a different lineup for testing.

    Camera

    The Arena is exactly one old 480x270 screen. Levels now show a Vector2i(960, 540) view, so the Arena is zoomed in until it fills the screen. It looks just as it always did, at 2x. The camera never moves, except to shake.

    Design notes

    • The Arena is a straight port of the TypeScript prototype's level, down to its baked sky, terrain and clouds.
    • Its fixed single screen came before the scrolling, Terraria-style levels. Burrow Test shows the newer model, and Level Parts explains how levels are built.

    Tunable parameters

    These are read straight from the game's files, so they are always the current values. Open the file in the Godot Inspector to change them.
