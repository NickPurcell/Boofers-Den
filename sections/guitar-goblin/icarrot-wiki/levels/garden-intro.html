    Work in progress

    The garden and the fall are done. Nothing loads the intro yet (the title still goes to the Arena), and the cave at the bottom of the shaft is a placeholder for the first burrow area.

    The Garden Intro is the calm before the dungeon. The carrots drop into a sunny vegetable garden, stroll across it, and jump into a hole that leads down into the Warm Burrow.

    What happens

    1. The garden: you land on grass under a blue sky with puffy clouds and a sun. Behind you are rolling hills with a little red barn, then a picket fence with leafy rows, sunflowers, a bean pole and a scarecrow.
    2. The giant carrot: right next to where you land, a huge carrot grows out of the soil. Only its fat orange shoulder shows above the grass, with a sleepy, blushing face and a big bushy leaf top that sways in the breeze. It's a plant, not an enemy: a nod to the heroes being carrots too.
    3. The walk: a few seconds of running across the garden, with the backdrop scrolling at different depths.
    4. The hole: a wide opening in the ground, with grass curling over its lips, roots dangling down it, and amber mushrooms glowing on both walls.
    5. The fall: jump in and the camera follows you down a deep shaft. Mushrooms glow past on the walls, the garden slides away above, and the blurred foreground grass sweeps past the camera. You land in a small burrow cave.

    Camera

    The camera aims at an offset of (0, -110) from your feet, well above the usual, so the sky and the garden fill most of the screen instead of the dirt under your feet. Fall faster than 300 px/s and it leads up to 260 px below you, so you can see the bottom coming. The giant carrot's leaves lean up to 0.06 radians each way, swaying 0.35 times a second. Enemy waves are switched off.

    What it's made of

    • Ground: a thin strip of the arena's grassy dirt over Warm Burrow soil, so the shaft walls turn into burrow dirt as you fall.
    • Backdrop: a sky fixed to the screen, then clouds, far hills, near hills and the garden rows, each scrolling a little faster than the one behind it.
    • Decorations: the giant carrot, its leaves, the hole's rims and the wall mushrooms are Props. These are sprites with an optional glow that blooms, and an optional sway. The art is baked by tools/garden/bake.gd.

    Design notes

    • The giant carrot sits right beside the spawn, so it's the first thing everyone sees.
    • The amber wall mushrooms use the Warm Burrow's glow-cap colours, a hint of where the hole leads.
