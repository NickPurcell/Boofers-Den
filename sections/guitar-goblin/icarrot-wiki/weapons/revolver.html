      1. Revolver

    The revolver is the gun every carrot starts with: one shot per click, tight and accurate, with a strong kick and a heavy shove on whatever it hits. It is the long-range option of the three.

    Handling

    It fires one shot per click, as fast as 0.14 s apart, with a very tight aim wobble of 1.1°. Bullets fly at 680 px/s. The cylinder holds 6 rounds rounds.

    Each shot flips the muzzle up by 25.8° and slides the gun back 2.5 px, settling quickly, and shakes the screen by 0.24.

    Damage

    Each bullet deals 11 hp on the body and 2 times that on a head.

    Distance Damage
    Up to 450 px Full
    By 750 px Down to 0.85 of full
    1100 px and beyond 0.7 of full

    That curve barely bites at the distances you see on screen, which is what sets it apart from the SMG and the Shotgun. Each hit shoves the enemy 1.45x times its normal shove, and lands with a screen thump of 0.13 (0.24 on a headshot).

    Reload

    The cylinder swings out, the spent brass tumbles to the ground one casing at a time, fresh rounds go in and it snaps shut, taking 0.5 s in all. See Reloading.

    Strategy

    • Aim for heads: the multiplier makes the revolver the best gun for picking off weak spots from across the screen.
    • Its heavy shove can push a charging enemy back off a ledge or out of slapping range.
    • It reloads fast, so it's a good gun to swap to when another one runs dry.

    Design notes

    The revolver is the precise, deliberate gun: slower than the SMG but each shot counts, with the strongest per-bullet knockback and hit shake of the three. Its falloff is gentle on purpose, so the Shotgun and SMG own close range while the revolver owns distance.
