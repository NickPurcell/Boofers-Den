      1. Energy ball

    Every carrot carries an energy ball instead of a frag grenade: a glowing orb with a white-hot core, a cyan body and a violet rim, shedding sparks and crackling with little arcs as it flies. It's lobbed, bounces around, and after a short fuse bursts in a ring of lightning that hurts every enemy nearby. It recharges by itself.

    Throwing

    Press G or the right mouse button to throw it at the crosshair. It leaves the hand at 320 px/s along the aim with an extra 110 px/s of lift, so even a flat throw arcs. It also keeps 0.5 of your own movement, so throwing while running or jumping carries it further.

    You hold 1 at a time. Each one recharges in 6 s, and the icon in the bottom-left Weapon HUD fills with light from the bottom as it does, then flashes and chimes when it's ready. With more than one charge, throws are at least 0.4 s apart.

    Flight and bounce

    The ball falls under 560 px/s² and bounces off the ground and walls, keeping 0.6 of its speed each time, with a springy zap. Each bounce on the floor keeps 0.7 of its sideways speed, so it rolls to a stop. Planks only stop it from above: a lob rises straight up through them.

    What it does on touching an enemy is set by on_enemy: by default it passes straight through them and keeps flying. It can also be set to bounce off (keeping 0.25 of its speed) or to burst on contact.

    The burst

    1.5 s after the throw it bursts. Over its last 0.9 s it swells and throbs faster and faster with rising blips, so everyone can see it's about to go.

    The burst is a quick flash, a shrinking plasma sphere, two shockwave rings and 9 lightning bolts thrown out to the edge of the radius, then a scatter of glowing motes.

    Damage

    Every enemy whose body is within 58 px of the burst is hit. The damage is 90 hp at the centre, falling to 0.35 of that at the edge, measured to the nearest point of each enemy's body, so big enemies are easy to catch. Each hit also shoves the enemy sideways, away from the burst: 6x its normal shove at the centre, less towards the edge.

    Teammates are never hurt. Your own ball doesn't hurt you either unless self_damage is set (currently 0 hp), but it does launch you if you're inside the radius, up to 320 px/s at the centre: a grenade jump. Throw it at your feet and jump.

    The burst shakes the screen by up to 0.75 right next to it, fading out to nothing by 420 px away.

    Strategy

    • Lob it into a group: with pass-through on, it sails through the front enemy and bursts in the middle of the pack.
    • Bounce it off a wall or roll it along the floor to reach an enemy around a corner or under a plank.
    • Use it to escape: a ball at your feet launches you up and away.

    Co-op

    The throw travels as one network message and every machine flies its own copy of the ball from it, so everyone sees the same arc and the same burst. Only the thrower's machine works out the damage and reports it to the host. Only the thrower is launched by their own ball.

    Design notes

    It started as a classic frag grenade and became an energy ball to fit the game's look: cartoon plasma instead of shrapnel. Every brightness in the burst (the flash, the core, the motes, the bolts and the throb before it goes) has its own slider, because several bright things clumped together bloom into a white blob very quickly.

    History

    • Added as a frag grenade with a fuse light and a fireball, then reworked into the energy ball.
    • Now passes through enemies by default instead of bursting on the first one it touches.
