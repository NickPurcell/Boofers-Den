      1. SMG

    The SMG fires as long as you hold the trigger: lots of small hits from a 32 rounds-round magazine. It shreds anything close and falls off fast with distance.

    Handling

    Hold the button to fire a round every 0.068 s. The aim wobbles by 4° per shot, and bullets fly at 720 px/s before vanishing after 340 px. A brass casing flicks out of the ejection port with every shot.

    The gun barely moves when it fires: a small kick of 1.5 px that settles before the next round, and a light screen shake of 0.07 per shot, so a long burst stays readable.

    Damage

    Each bullet deals 5 hp on the body and 1.6 times that on a head.

    Distance Damage
    Up to 110 px Full
    By 180 px Down to 0.45 of full
    260 px and beyond 0.12 of full

    Each hit shoves the enemy only 0.4x times its normal shove, so a stream pins a target in place instead of pushing it out of range. Hits shake the screen by just 0.04 (0.07 on a headshot), because so many land each second.

    Reload

    The empty magazine drops, a fresh one slaps in and the bolt is racked, taking 1.1 s. See Reloading.

    Strategy

    • Get close. Inside 110 px it keeps pace with the Revolver without needing careful aim; past 260 px it barely scratches.
    • Hold it on a target that's charging you: the low knockback keeps it coming into your fire rather than bouncing it away.
    • The big magazine covers a whole fight, but the reload is long, so swap to another gun rather than reload mid-fight.

    Design notes

    The SMG is the hose: low damage per hit, very high rate of fire, fast falloff. Its knockback and hit shake are low on purpose, because at this rate of fire the Revolver's values would juggle enemies away and shake the screen constantly.
