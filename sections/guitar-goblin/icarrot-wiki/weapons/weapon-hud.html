    Weapon HUD

    The bottom-left readout of your energy ball's recharge, the rounds in your gun and its name.

    The bottom-left corner of the screen shows your own weapons, left to right:

    1. The Energy ball. A dark, unlit shell fills with light from the bottom as the ball recharges, with a bright line marking the fill. When it's ready it flashes and chimes, then sits lit. With more than one charge, there's one icon per charge.
    2. Ammo. One pip per round in the gun in hand: brass bullets for the Revolver, red shells for the Shotgun, and thin ticks for the SMG's big magazine (any gun with more than 12 rounds). Spent rounds show as dark slots. While reloading the pips dim and refill from left to right as the reload progresses; when the gun is empty, the slots pulse red.
    3. The gun's name, dimmed while reloading.

    The controls line sits in the bottom-right corner (H hides it), and your health bar is at the top centre. The reload also shows as a bar under the crosshair (see Reloading).

    Look

    The whole HUD is drawn at 2x, inset from the corner by margin. The energy-ball icon is drawn dim by default so it doesn't bloom: it fills at a brightness of 0.6, sits at 0.7 once ready, and flashes up to 1.4 for 0.4 s the moment a ball is ready. The icon's white core is drawn a little brighter than these, so raising them much past the defaults makes the icon glow.

    Tunable parameters

    These are read straight from the game's files, so they are always the current values. Open the file in the Godot Inspector to change them.
