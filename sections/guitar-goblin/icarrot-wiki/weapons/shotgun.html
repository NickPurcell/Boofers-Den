      1. Shotgun

    The shotgun trades range for a huge point-blank hit. Every pull of the trigger fans out a cloud of pellets; at close range they all land and add up to one big number, while at a distance most of them miss and the rest fall off sharply.

    Handling

    Each trigger pull fires 8 pellets in a fan 17.2° wide, plus an aim wobble of 1.1° for the whole blast. Each pellet strays up to 0.35 of its slot from an even fan and flies a little slower than the last (up to 0.2 slower), so the cloud stretches out as it travels. Pellets fly at 560 px/s and vanish after 300 px.

    The gun holds 6 rounds shells and is pumped after every shot: the pump racks back, a red hull flies out, and it snaps home. Shots are 0.75 s apart.

    Firing shoves the shooter back along the aim at 170 px/s. On the ground it's a short slide; aimed at the floor in mid-air, it's a small boost upward.

    Damage

    Every pellet deals 7 hp on the body and 1.5 times that on a head. The headshot bonus is smaller than the Revolver's, since a blast at a head lands many pellets at once.

    Pellets that hit the same enemy within 0.05 s of each other add up into a single hit: one damage number, one flinch, one message to the host. A point-blank blast reads as one big gold or white number instead of a flicker of small ones.

    Each pellet also shoves its target 0.7x times the enemy's normal shove, and the shoves add up, so a point-blank blast knocks an enemy back hardest.

    Reload

    Shells go in one at a time, then the pump is racked. A full reload takes 1.4 s. See Reloading.

    Strategy

    • Close the distance. Inside 60 px every pellet lands at full damage; a blast that catches a small enemy's head can drop it outright.
    • Use the self-push: a blast at the floor mid-jump gains height, and a blast while backing away opens distance from a charger.
    • Swap away rather than reload mid-fight: the shell-by-shell reload is the slowest of the three.

    Co-op

    The fan is seeded by the shot itself, so every machine draws exactly the same blast from a single network message. The shooter works out the final damage, including falloff and pellet merging, and reports it to the host.

    Design notes

    Shooters usually limit shotguns two ways: the spread (fewer pellets land the further away the target is) and damage falloff per pellet. This one uses both, with the spread doing most of the work and a falloff that only really starts past 60 px. The fan is an even pattern with a little seeded jitter rather than pure randomness, so blasts are consistent (as in Destiny), and the same on every machine.

    History

    • Added as the third gun, along with knockback, self-push and pellet merging, which the other guns now share through their own sliders.
    • The pump now racks visibly after every shot, with the hull ejecting on the back-stroke.
