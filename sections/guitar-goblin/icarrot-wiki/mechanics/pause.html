    Pause

    Pause
    Keys
    Esc or P
    Who it pauses
    Everyone in the session

    Esc or P freezes the game for everyone in the squad. Press either again to carry on.

    What it does

    Press Esc or P during a level and the game freezes: carrots, enemies, bullets, effects and sounds all stop where they are, under a dimmed screen with a "PAUSED" card. Press either key again to pick up exactly where you left off. For now that's all it does: there's no menu yet.

    The global keys still work while paused: C toggles the CRT look, B the bloom, and M mutes.

    Co-op

    Pausing freezes the game for the whole squad, not just you, and anyone can resume it. Someone who joins while the game is paused sees the level fade in, then freezes with everyone else.

    Design notes

    A raid game would normally never pause online. iCarrot pauses everyone for now because the squad is usually friends playing together, and it makes testing and tuning easier. The pause card's look is tunable on scenes/pause_overlay.tscn.

    Tunable parameters

    These are read straight from the game's files, so they are always the current values. Open the file in the Godot Inspector to change them.
