    Shield and health

    Every carrot has 130 pts shield over 70 hp health. Hits drain the shield first and only reach health once it's broken; a hit bigger than the shield that's left spills the rest into health. Enemy attack damage is on that scale, so a 15 hp slap from a Zombie costs you that many points. Both refill on their own a while after the last hit, and at zero health you're knocked out. Health covers the shield breaking, recharging and being downed.

    Enemies have their own health, from a few dozen for a regular enemy to thousands for a boss. Each enemy's page lists it.

    Hurting enemies

    Every gun has a body damage per bullet (per pellet for the Shotgun). Two things change it:

    • Range. Bullets do full damage out to the gun's full range, then fade toward a smaller fraction, and on down to a far fraction past that. Close-range guns like the SMG fall off hard; the Revolver barely does. Each gun's page has its numbers.
    • Headshots. Every enemy has a head hitbox (usually the top of its body; some enemies have a weak spot instead). A bullet that lands there does the gun's headshot multiplier times the damage, shows a gold number, and kicks the screen a little harder.

    The number that pops up is the damage that hit landed. Shotgun pellets that hit the same enemy in one blast add up into a single number.

    Hits also shove the enemy back. How far depends on the enemy (each has its own shot knockback) and on the gun (its knockback multiplier). Pellets shove separately, so a point-blank shotgun blast knocks hardest. The Grenade's blast damages everything in its radius.

    Immune enemies

    Some enemies can't be hurt at times: a Ginger Shade never can, and The Ginger is shielded until the squad unlocks it. A bullet that hits one shows IMMUNE instead of a number and does nothing. A boss's health bar shows when it's immune; see Enemy names and boss bars.

    Getting hurt

    Enemy attacks come in three kinds:

    • Contact and melee (a Zombie slap, a Potato Bull charge): fixed damage, plus a kick away from the enemy.
    • Shots (kernels, spores, fireballs): if one strikes your carrot's head, it does 2x times its damage and the screen shakes harder. Keep your head down.
    • Blasts hurt you if any part of your carrot is inside the blast radius, and throw you away from the centre.

    Every hit shows a damage number over your carrot, which your squad sees too: blue when only your shield took it, red when it reached your health.

    Dodging: i-frames

    For the first 0.2 s of a Dash your carrot can't be hurt. Shots, blasts, slaps and charges all pass straight through, with a sparkle and a whiff to show the dodge. Your carrot's rim glows in its accent colour while it's untouchable. Learning each enemy's telegraph and dashing through the attack is the core defensive skill.

    Co-op

    Each player's machine decides its own hits, so what you see is what counts:

    • Your bullets: your machine checks what they hit and tells the host, which takes the health off the enemy and announces deaths.
    • Enemy attacks on you: your machine checks your carrot against them and takes the damage.

    Everyone sees everyone's damage numbers. See Co-op.
