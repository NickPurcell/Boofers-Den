      1. Ammo bricks

    Every carrot carries three guns (see Weapons), and each uses one of three kinds of ammo, the way Destiny does it:

    Ammo Gun Most you can carry You start with Brick
    Normal (brass) SMG Never runs out – –
    Special (green) Shotgun 20 rounds 10 rounds 6 rounds
    Heavy (purple) Rocket Launcher 5 rounds 2 rounds 2 rounds

    "Most you can carry" counts what's loaded in the gun: a shotgun at its maximum has its tube full and the rest in reserve. You start a level with some special and heavy ammo but not a full load, so the first bricks matter.

    Reserves and reloading

    A gun is loaded from its reserve. The SMG's reserve never runs out, so it can always reload. The shotgun and rocket launcher reload only what their reserve still holds: a shotgun with three shells left in reserve thumbs in three and stops. With nothing in reserve the gun can't reload at all; pulling the trigger gives a dry click and the ammo on the Weapon HUD blinks red. The gun stays selectable, so you can swap back to it once you've picked up a brick. See Reloading.

    Bricks

    Every enemy that dies may drop an ammo brick: a glowing crate in the ammo's colour that pops out of the body, bounces, and sits on the ground bobbing under a beacon of light you can spot across the screen. At most one brick drops per kill.

    • Chances: each kill has a 0.04 chance of a heavy brick, and otherwise a 0.14 chance of a special one. Each kind can have its own chances per enemy.
    • Bad-luck protection: every kill that doesn't drop a brick of a kind raises that kind's chance by 0.02 (special) or 0.008 (heavy), until one drops.
    • Bosses always drop a heavy brick.
    • Picking up: walk over it. It gives its rounds to the gun that uses that ammo, never past the maximum, with a thunk, a burst of sparks, a little bob and a "+N" on the HUD. A brick for a gun that's already full stays where it is for later.
    • Disappearing: a brick lies there for 30 s, blinking faster over its last 5 s.

    Co-op

    Bricks are per player, as in Destiny. The host rolls each drop when it applies the kill and tells every machine; each machine then shows its own copy of the brick, which only its own player can pick up. Partners never fight over ammo, and nothing about a pickup needs to be sent. Each player keeps their own ammo counts. A player who joins late doesn't see bricks dropped before they came.

    Loadout

    Which guns you carry is the loadout, styles/loadout.tres: the SMG, Shotgun and Rocket Launcher, on keys 1, 2 and 3 (Q cycles them). The Revolver is still in the game but not in the current loadout; drag it into the loadout in the Inspector to carry it again.

    Testing

    --drops=1 makes every kill drop a brick, and --ammo=special:N,heavy:N (or --ammo=inf) sets the starting ammo. The test bot never runs out unless --ammo is given.
