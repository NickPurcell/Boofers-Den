    Glowshroom Grotto

    Glowshroom Grotto
    Mood
    Dreamy, mysterious, magical

    Look

    A deep cavern where nearly all the light comes from living things. The earth, roots and backdrop are cool indigo, violet and deep teal, and three candy colours glow out of it: cyan, magenta and lime toadstools, moss, buds and spores. Every walkable top has a bright teal moss lip, so the playfield sits clearly in front of the near-black backdrop.

    Orange is the only warm colour on screen, so the carrots always pop.

    It comes after the Overgrown Vault and before the Compost Depths.

    What it draws

    Ground blocks (Solid). Muted indigo-violet earth under a bright teal moss lip with hanging drips. The sides are inked dark, and the underside catches a faint light, as if lit by a glowing floor below. The fill is a calm mottle that darkens with depth, with pebbles, buried cyan crystals, rootlets ending in little glowing bulbs, and the odd pink worm curled in a pocket. The earth is kept dull on purpose so it never competes with the glow.

    Jump-through ledges (Plank). A pale lavender branch with a lit top and grain. The left end is a cut end showing pink growth rings; the right end tapers into a curly vine tendril. The odd lime vine is lashed round it, and rootlets with glowing bulbs hang below.

    Mushroom platforms (Shroom).

    • Cap: a glossy candy toadstool in cyan, magenta or lime. The colour is picked from the node's name, so a row of mushrooms comes out mixed. A dome, a dark crease, then a lit brim that sticks out past the dome and curls down at the ends, over a lens of gills and a few luminous drips. That light-dark-light banding stops it reading as a pill.
    • Glow: the gill blades, drips, spot cores and a glint, each in the cap's own colour.
    • Stem: pale lavender, tinted by the gills' glow at the top and falling into shadow further down, with a frilly ring in the cap's colour, a gentle bow and a bulb at the base. Tall stems bow more and dim more, so a forest of them recedes.

    Backdrop. Three baked layers, seamless in both directions.

    • Far: flat deep indigo with faint hanging drapery, soft pools of light where distant colonies of glowshrooms grow, and a sprinkle of glow-worms. This layer barely moves.
    • Mid: giant twisted root trunks running the full height, sagging roots slung between them with curtains of rootlets, and dim toadstools growing out of the trunks at an angle. Everything is lit from below: dark tops, cool-lit undersides. It drifts a little more.
    • Near: two thick twisted roots with leaves and clusters of dim toadstools jutting out, two hanging vines with dim bulbs, and drifting spore motes. It moves the most of the three.

    Foreground. Blurred silhouettes in front of the playfield: a hanging clump with roots and a leafy vine ending in a bulb, one long dangling vine, a root running across with a curtain of rootlets, a side wall with a toadstool growing out sideways, a bottom lip of mounds and little toadstools, and a tall cluster of toadstools rising from the bottom edge, the grotto's signature shape.

    Using it in a level

    • On a Solid set look to GROTTO, on a Plank set look to GROTTO, and on a Shroom set biome to GROTTO. Rename a Shroom to change its colour. See Level Parts.
    • Baked files are in art/baked/grotto_*: grotto_bg_far, grotto_bg_mid, grotto_bg_near, and the foreground pieces grotto_fg_hang, _vine, _curtain, _side, _bottom and _cluster, each with a _blur version for the game. scenes/grotto_test.tscn shows the setup.
    • To re-bake, run tools/underground/glowshroom_grotto/bake.gd as a headless Godot script.

    Design notes

    • The carrots must pop. The cave is all cool colours, so orange carrots are the only warm thing on screen. Only living things glow; the earth, wood and backdrop stay dark so the glow has something to bloom against. The teal moss on every walkable top doubles as a readability line.
    • Nothing in the backdrop may look like a platform. The playfield owns the bright moss tops, so backdrop shapes keep dark tops. Distant mushrooms are small, dark, domed and tilted, standing in soft light pools on hills that fade out downwards; the only light back there is pools, undersides and pinprick glows.
    • The foreground blur is dithered. Edges soften in a few see-through steps with an ordered dither. Pure black silhouettes vanish against a black ceiling, so each one carries a cool "underlight" rim on the side facing the glowing floor. Roots are drawn thick so the blur doesn't eat them.
    • Known weak spots: stems are plain; the glow doesn't animate yet (a slow pulse on spots and bulbs, and drifting spores, would suit it); and very large ground fills can show a faint repeat.
