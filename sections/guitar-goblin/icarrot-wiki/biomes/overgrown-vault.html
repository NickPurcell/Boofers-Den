    Look

    The heroes dig into the vault of an ancient vegetable kingdom, and roots have got there first. It should feel like the entrance hall of a raid: a colonnade of arches recedes into green darkness, and at the far end a sealed round vault door carries the kingdom's carrot crest and glows green-gold.

    Up front the playfield is warm and bright with cartoon outlines: packed brown dirt with sage moss lips, twisted roots lashed round old stone brackets, and fat ivory mushrooms with glowing gold gills. The tone stays friendly: the glow is gold rather than toxic green, the roots end in little pig-tail curls, and the mushrooms have big freckles.

    It comes after the Warm Burrow and before the Glowshroom Grotto.

    What it draws

    Ground blocks (Solid). Packed brown dirt with a sage moss lip along the top that drips down and spills over the top corners, an outline round the sides and underside, and a calm mottle that darkens with depth. Buried in it, sparsely: broken chunks of old vault wall with bitten corners, gold ore veins, carved tablets bearing the carrot crest, bark roots ending in pig-tail curls, pebbles and rootlets.

    Jump-through ledges (Plank). A twisted root, lit along the top like a rope. One end is lashed to a carved stone bracket: a lit block with a groove, a stepped corbel underneath with a tiny gold carrot crest, and two root lashings. The other end tapers off and curls down into a pig-tail. Longer ledges get old stone blocks lashed in along the span, and moss lumps, rootlets and glow-berries hang underneath.

    Mushroom platforms (Shroom).

    • Cap: a fat ivory dome with big tan freckles and a full-width brim that sticks out past the dome and curls down at the ends, over a lens of gold gills fanning in towards the stem.
    • Glow: the gill stripes and the spore pores in the bigger freckles bloom gold.
    • Stem: ivory, lit on the left, with a frilly ring under the gills, a gentle bend and a fat bulb at the base.

    Backdrop. Three baked layers, seamless in both directions.

    • Far: a two-storey colonnade of round arches in the dark, two dim sealed niche doors with faint rays, and drifting dust. Every line is a curve or a pillar. This layer barely moves.
    • Mid: ruined fluted columns wound with roots, two arches broken at the keystone (only their steep feet remain), a chain ending in a crest medallion, and dim glow-berries. It drifts a little more.
    • Near: two thick roots with mossy clods, and a chunk of masonry caught in one. It moves the most of the three.

    Vault door. A one-off set piece for an arena's back wall: a great round stone door in an arch, with a ring of runes, sealing seams and the carrot crest, overgrown with moss. A separate glow image sits on top so the runes, seams and crest bloom.

    Foreground. Blurred silhouettes in front of the playfield: a hanging mossy clump with roots ending in curls, sagging vines with a curtain of roots, a chain ending in the crest medallion, a side wall of broken masonry with roots crawling over it, a bottom lip with grass tufts, mushroom silhouettes and a fallen carved block, and a broken column standing up from the bottom.

    Using it in a level

    • On a Solid set look to VAULT, on a Plank set look to VAULT, and on a Shroom set biome to VAULT. See Level Parts.
    • Baked files are in art/baked/vault_*: vault_bg_far, vault_bg_mid, vault_bg_near, the door (vault_door and vault_door_glow), and the foreground pieces vault_fg_hang, _vines, _chain, _side, _lip and _column, each with a _blur version for the game.
    • Put the door behind the level geometry with its foot tucked behind the floor, and the glow image on top with a modulate brighter than white so it blooms. scenes/vault_test.tscn shows the whole setup, including where the hanging foreground pieces go: along the ceiling, or they drift mid-screen.
    • To re-bake, run tools/underground/overgrown_vault/bake.gd as a headless Godot script.

    Design notes

    • The carrots must pop. Each layer is a step darker and flatter than the one in front: far is teal-black, mid is sage, near is warm brown, and only the playfield is bright with outlines. Gold is the only colour that blooms in the playfield; the ivory caps are bright but stay unbloomed, or they would wash out.
    • Nothing in the backdrop may look like a platform. An earlier far wall had a band behind the arches that lined up into a shelf, so it was removed. The mid arches are broken so they have no flat tops, and the near roots run up and down.
    • The foreground blur is dithered. Edges soften in a few see-through steps with an ordered dither, with a faint green rim light on the lit side, and roots and curls are drawn thick so they survive. Hanging pieces have rounded tops, because a flat top clipped by the frame read as a beam.
    • Known weak spots: big flat dirt faces are calm but plain; the far niche doors repeat every tile; and the gold veins and crest grooves in the ground can't bloom, because ground blocks have no glow layer. The door would be a great raid moment if it pulsed or opened.
