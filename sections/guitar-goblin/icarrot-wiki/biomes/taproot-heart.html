    Look

    The final boss chamber of the dungeon, inside the root system of a colossal ancient carrot. Huge amber taproots twist up into a glowing heart above the top of the screen: a cut carrot's cross-section, ringed, with a white-hot star at its core. The heart lights the ceiling brightly, and the light fades off so the play band stays in plum-dark shadow.

    The ground is maroon soil with a cool lilac moss rim, the platforms are pale root-wood, and the mushrooms have dusty rose caps. It is the last stop after the Compost Depths.

    What it draws

    Ground blocks (Solid). Mottled plum soil that darkens with depth, crossed by faint strata, under a lilac moss lip with drips that creep a little way down the sides. The sides have a warm rim and the underside is dark. Fine root hairs thread down from the moss, thick buried roots wander through, and the cut ends of roots show their growth rings, among pebbles, clods and crumbs.

    Jump-through ledges (Plank). A gnarled branch of pale root-wood whose fibres twist like rope, with tapered tips curling down at the ends, a lumpy knotted underside, little forks dangling off it, root hairs, and the odd glowing pink root bud.

    Mushroom platforms (Shroom).

    • Cap: a big dusty rose parasol with large cream spots, scalloped lobes and a brim that curls down at the ends, over a deep lens of gills. Its proportions grow with its width, so wide caps hold their own next to the giant roots.
    • Glow: the gill blades and the spot cores bloom a soft pink.
    • Stem: cream blushing pink, with a frilly pink skirt under the gills, pores, a gentle bend and a flared foot with little root toes.

    Backdrop. Three baked layers, seamless in both directions, plus the heart.

    • Far: dark plum soil with soft mottling and a forest of distant thin taproots, barely warmer than the soil. This layer barely moves.
    • Mid: the giant ringed amber taproots plunging down through the chamber as continuous columns, shaded like cylinders lit from above, with growth rings, a slow spiral groove and a thin hot rim on the side facing the heart. Their bodies stay deep maroon. It drifts a little more.
    • Near: two dark roots closer to the camera with strong warm rims, framing the playfield. It moves the most of the three.
    • Heart: placed once above the boss chamber, not tiled. The glowing cross-section, held by dark rim-lit roots that dither out into the dark, in a translucent halo. A second image adds a warm wash of light over everything round it, so nearby roots and soil pick up its glow. In the test level the heart pulses slowly.

    Foreground. Blurred silhouettes in front of the playfield: a hanging clump with roots and a ringed taproot, one long dangling taproot, a root running across with a curtain of rootlets, a top corner overhang with a dangling whiskered clod, a side wall with roots poking in, and a bottom lip with root loops and parasol mushroom silhouettes.

    Using it in a level

    • On a Solid set look to TAPROOT, on a Plank set look to TAPROOT, and on a Shroom set biome to TAPROOT. See Level Parts.
    • Baked files are in art/baked/taproot_*: taproot_bg_far, taproot_bg_mid, taproot_bg_near, the heart (taproot_heart_glow and the additive taproot_heart_light), and the foreground pieces taproot_fg_hang, _taproot, _curtain, _corner, _side and _bottom, each with a _blur version for the game.
    • The heart goes in its own Parallax2D that moves with the mid layer, over the mid backdrop, with a modulate a little brighter than white so its core blooms. scenes/taproot_test.tscn shows the full setup, including the pulse.
    • To re-bake, run tools/underground/taproot_heart/bake.gd as a headless Godot script.

    Design notes

    • The carrots must pop, and this is the hardest room for it, because the whole backdrop is orange-gold. So the light falls off steeply from the heart: the top of the screen glows and the play band sinks into dark maroon, leaving the carrots a few steps brighter than anything behind them. The taproot bodies stay maroon and only their thin rims run hot. The ground's moss is cool lilac instead of orange, so walk lines separate from the background by hue as well as brightness; the ledges are pale bone and the mushrooms rose, so neither is orange.
    • Nothing in the backdrop may look like a platform. Every taproot is a full-height column, and side roots leave the columns steeply, never near flat.
    • The foreground blur is dithered. Edges soften in a few see-through steps with an ordered dither, with a warm red rim on the side facing the heart. Hanging clods have rounded tops, so they still read as root clumps when their top scrolls into view.
    • Known weak spots: the foreground silhouettes vanish where the backdrop is already dark (the top corners); the mid taproots' grooves and rings are a bit mechanical; and the heart could use a cartoon face or eye for the boss reveal.
