    Health

    Each carrot has 100 hp health, shown as the bar at the top middle of the screen. Enemy attacks are on the same scale, so you can read an attack's damage as a share of your bar (each enemy page lists its damage, for example the Zombie's slap).

    When a hit lands, the bar drops and a pale chunk briefly shows how much the hit took before sliding away. The bar pulses red when you're low.

    Taking a hit

    • The carrot flashes white, squashes, and is knocked back by the hit.
    • Your screen shakes and the damage pops up as a number over your head.
    • Headshots: enemy shots that land on the top of your carrot do 2x damage.
    • Grace period: for 1 s after a hit you can't be hurt again, and the carrot blinks to show it.

    A dash makes you invincible too (see Dash).

    Recharging

    If you go 3 s without being hit, your health starts to refill at 18 hp/s until it's full. Any hit restarts the wait, so the way to heal is to break off and stay safe for a moment.

    Knocked down

    At 0 health the carrot is down. It tips over onto its back with its boots kicking in the air, stars circling its head, and drops its gun. A downed carrot can't move or shoot and stays down.

    When every player is down, the squad is wiped: a banner shows, and 3.5 s later the host restarts the level for everyone.

    Co-op

    Each player's own machine tracks their health and decides when they're hit, so a hit always matches what that player saw. Health and being down are streamed to everyone, so you see your teammates' damage numbers, blinking and knock-downs.

    Design notes

    Fast recharge and no healing items keep fights about positioning: take a few hits, back off, come back. The short grace period after a hit stops a crowd from draining you all at once.
