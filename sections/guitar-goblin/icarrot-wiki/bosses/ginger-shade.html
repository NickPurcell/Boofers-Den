    A ghostly projection of The Ginger: a translucent purple-black silhouette of the ginger-root sorcerer, with a crooked root hood, a knobbly body, a dangling rootlet beard, glowing purple eyes, a flickering glowing rim and a wispy trailing tail. It is much smaller than its master. The Ginger can't leave the hub, so it sends the shade into the boss rooms to make the squad's life harder there.

    It can't be hurt. Shots that hit it show "IMMUNE" and do nothing. It is a nuisance to dodge, not an enemy to kill.

    Behaviour

    The Ginger brings in exactly one shade as soon as it appears. From then on the shade picks its own room:

    • Haunting. While any boss room's fight is on, it haunts one of them: the active room with the most players in it (ties go to the first room). Once there, it sticks with that room while its fight lasts, even if the squad moves about.
    • Travelling. It streaks to its room straight through the walls at 900 px/s, as a faint see-through streak, then phases in over 0.9 s beside the player nearest the room's middle. It can't attack while phasing in, and can't be hit until it's mostly solid.
    • Floating. In its room it drifts after the nearest standing player there at up to 70 px/s, hovering 110 px to their side with its tail 70 px above their feet, wandering lazily and bobbing. It keeps 24 px inside the room's walls and ceiling. Touching it does no harm.
    • Moving on. When its room's boss dies (the room clears), it fades out with a wail over 1 s. If another room's fight is on it heads there; otherwise it goes dormant: invisible, untouchable and silent, parked by The Ginger in the hub until a room's fight starts again.

    Its name tag and little health bar show only while it's properly in a room. It never fights in the hub.

    Attacks

    Bolt fan

    Its first volley comes 1.4 s after it phases in, then one every 2.6 s to 3.8 s. It only starts a volley at a player within 520 px with a clear line of sight.

    Step Lasts What you see
    Wind-up 0.9 s It raises its arms, a spectral orb swells between its hands, and a dotted aim line flickers at its target. Its eyes burn brighter. The aim follows you, but only slowly (at 2.5 rad/s). This is the telegraph.
    Volley instant It looses 3 slow purple bolts in a fan, 14 ° apart, along the aim line.

    Each bolt flies at 165 px/s for up to 3 s and deals 10 damage with very little knockback. Bolts fizzle on walls and when they leave the room.

    Dodge: when the aim line appears, move sideways; the aim lags behind you. The bolts are slow, so jumping over or slipping between them works too, and a dash carries you straight through.

    Strategy

    • Don't shoot it. It's immune; spend the ammo on the room's boss and adds.
    • Keep an eye out for its aim line while you fight the room boss. Its bolts are light, but they add up and can knock you into something worse.
    • The quicker the room's boss dies, the sooner it leaves.
    • A squad split across rooms can choose where it goes: it haunts the room with the most players.

    Headshots

    Its head box is its face, near the top of its body, a little forward on the side it's facing. As it is immune, a headshot does nothing more than a body shot.

    Co-op

    The host chooses its room and target and streams its state and aim to everyone. Each wind-up and volley is an event every machine plays out, and every machine flies the bolts itself. Each machine checks only its own carrot, and only while that carrot is inside the bolt's room: players in other rooms or the hub are never hit by it. Dash i-frames dodge everything.

    Death

    It can't be killed by players. When The Ginger dies it takes the shade with it: the shade dissolves over 1.8 s with a long wail, and any bolts still flying fizzle out.

    Appearances

    • Burrow Encounter: it haunts the boss rooms while their fights are on. The three room bosses, Sweet Potato, Red Corn and Ghost Pepper, each drop a key orb that goes on a hub pedestal; those orbs unlock The Ginger.
    • Outside an encounter (the arena, for testing) it haunts the whole level.

    Design notes

    The Ginger is stuck in the hub, so without the shade the room fights would have nothing to do with the main boss. The shade ties them together: the room fights feel like part of one raid, with the big boss reaching into every room. It is immune so it can't distract from the room's real target, and it is light and well telegraphed so it adds pressure without taking over the fight.
