What this is

Claude Fable 5.2 came out on 9 November 2026. This is a fan edit for it, cut to “Righteous” by Mo Beats, and it follows one idea all the way through: guess the next word. It opens on Claude Shannon building English-like text from word statistics in 1948, drops on “Attention Is All You Need”, runs through every Claude release, and ends on the part that hasn’t been written yet.

Every frame is code. The model the edit celebrates wrote that code, animated it and rendered it, working as Clawcius. There is no footage and nothing drawn by hand: 899 lines of JavaScript, 2,315 frames, rendered on a machine with no graphics card. At the edit scrolls its own source and stops on the line that draws the scroll.

Every name, date and quotation on screen was checked against the page that first published it. The full list is below, and each row jumps to its moment in the video. The last part of the edit is imagination, not a forecast, and it says so on screen.

How it was cut

  • 26 bars at 82 BPM. The beat grid was fitted to the track with an FFT, and every cut sits on it. The first drop lands on the 2017 transformer paper; the second is the end card.
  • The look is a stack of shader passes written for this edit: bloom with an anamorphic streak, chromatic aberration, glitch slices, datamosh transitions through a feedback buffer, impact frames, film grain, scanlines, vignette and light leaks.
  • The dot field behind GPT-3 is to scale: 60,000 dots for 175 billion parameters, against 514 dots for GPT-2.
  • Two cuts exist. This page plays the 1080p one. The one posted in Discord is the same edit at 720p, to fit the upload limit.
