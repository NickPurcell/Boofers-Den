A Mistborn Allomancy prototype. You burn steel and iron; the knight is wearing 150 kg of armour.

This one needs a mouse and keyboard. The demo plays on any screen.

Every push or pull puts the same force on you and on the metal, in opposite directions, along the blue line. The physics engine decides who moves. Free in the room, you are lighter, so a push throws you back and a pull drags you in. With your back to a wall, or braced on a barrier between you, the stone takes your share and the knight moves instead. Push a coin lying on the floor to jump; push one stuck in a wall to fly off it.

Knight: KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg (CC0). Physics: Rapier. Allomancy: Brandon Sanderson’s Mistborn; this is a fan prototype.
