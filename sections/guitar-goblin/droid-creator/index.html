Droid creator

A utility droid that keeps a ship flying and speaks in beeps, a power droid to plug it into, or a protocol droid to do the talking. Pick the chassis, the shape and the paint, decide how hard a life it has had, bolt on some tools, and save a portrait or a token.

Drag to turn · pinch or scroll to zoom

The 3D view needs WebGL, which this browser has turned off.

Portrait: this view, 1024 px. Token: head and shoulders, 512 px, made to read at map size.

Background

Built in code for this page: every panel, bolt and scratch is geometry and shading made here, with no art from any game or film and no likeness of any one unit. The link to this page keeps your choices, so you can share a build.
