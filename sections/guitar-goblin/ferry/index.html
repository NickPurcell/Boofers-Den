    How this works

    Colours come from real drive times, not the circles. Each town’s drive to every terminal and to downtown Napa was routed once with OSRM on OpenStreetMap roads () and saved with the page. Those are free-flow times with no traffic. Rush multiplies the drive to the terminal by 1.35; Napa stays free-flow, since that trip is for weekends.

    The circles are only a guide: minutes × the average straight-line speed from that terminal (or Napa), fitted from the same table. A town across the water can sit inside a circle and stay grey.

    Door to desk = drive + 10 min to park, walk to the boat and board + the scheduled weekday morning ride + the last mile from the Ferry Building to Salesforce Plaza (walk 15 min or scooter 3 min). No wait for the next boat is included, so check the timetable for how often it runs.
