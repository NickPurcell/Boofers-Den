How long it takes

Dwell time is the product, and it falls out of the path length. 572 feet at a walking pace:

Strolling, barely stopping0.75 ft/s12.7 min
Stopping for photographs0.55 ft/s17.3 min
Lingering0.40 ft/s23.8 min

That brackets the twenty minutes the plan assumes. If it runs short on the night, the fix is lighting and sound rather than more corridor — people slow down in the dark.

Why there are emergency doors in the middle of the maze. With only the entry and the far exit, the worst point on this route is 265 feet from either of them. The travel-distance limit for a sprinklered Group A occupancy is 250 feet. The plan fails on that alone.

Two doors punched through the outer wall mid-run bring the worst case to about 150 feet. They read as ordinary sealed doors and release on alarm, so they cost nothing atmospherically until the moment they are needed. This is exactly the sort of thing plan review catches, and it is cheaper to draw now than to cut into a finished wall in October.

Why this shape

Serpentine, because path length is the scarce thing

A 6,000 sf box is not big. Folding a corridor back on itself at a 10 ft pitch buys 572 ft of walk out of 80 ft of building — roughly seven times the longest straight line available. Nothing else gets close for the money.

Rooms hang off the run, they do not sit in it

The three rooms open off the corridor rather than interrupting it. Someone stopped for a photograph is out of the through-route, so the group behind is not held up by them. Put a photo room in the corridor itself and you have built a bottleneck on purpose.

Two ends, deliberately far apart

Entry bottom-right, exit top-left. Nobody leaving walks back past anybody arriving, which matters more than it sounds: seeing the next group destroys the isolation the whole thing is selling.

What this is not. A layout study, not a construction drawing. Wall thicknesses are nominal, no structure or services are shown, and the real building will have columns, restrooms and an existing partition grid that none of this knows about. It is the shape to take to a space, not a set of instructions to build from.
