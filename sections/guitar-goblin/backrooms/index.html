The Backrooms

A walkthrough liminal-space attraction for the North Bay. Timed entry, no actors, twenty minutes, and you come out with photographs. This is the working file — the outline lives here and each piece gets its own page as it gets sharpened.

Where it stands

Three decisions are made and hold up under the numbers. Everything else is open.

Take an office, not a warehouse. The Backrooms look is a suspended ceiling grid, and you may not hang a solid ceiling under a sprinkler head. An office already has the grid with the heads through it, plus exit signs, alarm, HVAC and restrooms. Office is ~2.5× the rent per square foot and still the cheaper answer.
Take about 6,000 sf, not 10,000. Throughput is set by how often you release a group, not by floor area. The 10,000 sf version loses about $36k even at a healthy 70% sell-through.
It is an assembly occupancy regardless of occupant load. There is no version small enough to escape the classification. The fire marshal conversation comes before the lease, not after.
