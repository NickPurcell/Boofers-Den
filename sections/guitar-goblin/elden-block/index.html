STUDY elden_block // field report

Minecraft × Elden Ring × Black Ops

— is it real, and could we build it?

Clawcius, 30 Sep 2026. For Nick’s PC. Offline only: the game launched without Easy Anti-Cheat, matchmaking blocked, a separate save.

Verdict Is it fake? Build Steve ↔ soldier Limits Proved

IVerdict

LIKELY REAL

Tobyn’s clip is likely real. It’s a heavily edited montage, and some of its claims go further than the footage shows, but the gameplay itself looks like a mod running in the game. “AI-assisted editing” is half right: the editing is real (about 20 takes, hard cuts, short dissolves, captions added afterwards), but nothing in the frames looks generated or composited.

BUILDABLE

Nick’s version is buildable, offline on retail Elden Ring. That means Minecraft blocks and mobs, plus a hotkey that swaps Steve for a COD-style soldier with a gun. The hard part is letting you stand on and build with blocks at scale, because no public tool creates Elden Ring collision.

Malware warning

Don’t run anything from github.com/eldenringminecraft/Elden-Ring-Minecraft-Mod. It was created on 30 Sep, isn’t Tobyn’s, and ships a 73 MB “installer” zip. It is almost certainly malware riding the hype.

IIIs it fake?

Against

The case against it is generic: “no source code”, “could be an AI render”, “spliced”, “green screen”. The one specific claim, that blocks sit “on top of nothing” and the boss bar draws behind the Minecraft HUD, is explained by the depth evidence below. Press coverage hedges (“if Jacobs’ claims hold up”); only one outlet mentions doubt, and it gives no source.

For

The case for it, from frames of his uploads:

  • Live phone footage. His explainer is a phone filming his MacBook: Elden Ring under CrossOver, the Minecraft HUD, a block being placed, the creative inventory and Elden Ring’s own menu, with his hands in front of the screen.

  • Game state stays consistent within each take.

    • Each Ray Gun hit on the Elden Beast adds 10 points and drains its boss bar with Elden Ring’s native yellow damage trail.
    • The AUG’s ammo goes from 0/270 to 29/240 after a reload.
    • Radahn’s hits take away Minecraft hearts with the hurt flash, and a golden apple refills them.
    • Elden Ring’s damage numbers appear next to Radahn’s bar.

  • Depth is right.

    • Tree roots hide a creeper’s foot.
    • Radahn’s light effects draw over Steve.
    • Mobs walk over uneven ground.

  • The game’s AI reacts. With the player far away, an Elden Ring enemy turns and swings at freshly spawned creepers, and one explodes beside it.

  • No signs of video generation. Textures, lettering, hotbar counts and which mob is which all stay stable from frame to frame.

Edited, not faked. The switch from first to third person at 0:19.8 is a dissolve in the edit, not an in-game camera move. Hotbar counts jump between takes, and the captions were added in the edit.

Claimed but not shown:

  • “All 1,600 items”: the creative search shows many real icons, but few of them are used.
  • “Working redstone”: a lever lights the dust, but the lamp beside it stays dark.
  • Building at scale: one cobblestone floor and one dirt pillar.

Not released: no code, no mod, no Windows build, and nobody has reproduced it. His only comment is “this ran on my Mac btw”.

How it’s probably built (inferred from the footage).

  • Drawn by the mod: blocks and mobs use flat Minecraft shading, cast no shadows and ignore Elden Ring’s lighting. Steve walks with Minecraft animations, not Tarnished ones. That points to the mod drawing them itself into Elden Ring’s frame, tested against its depth buffer, with the player model hidden.
  • Stand-ins for the AI: enemies attacking creepers suggests invisible Elden Ring characters stand in for the mobs.
  • No soldier model: Black Ops mode is first-person guns plus a Zombies-style HUD.

IIIHow we’d build it

Loader. me3 launches the game directly without Easy Anti-Cheat, blocks matchmaking by default, uses a separate save, and works under CrossOver. ModEngine2 is archived. The code is a Rust DLL on fromsoftware-rs, pinned to game version 1.17.1. That library already covers:

  • the world’s characters and their teams;
  • debug character spawns and runtime asset spawns;
  • projectile spawns and raycasts.

Smithbox handles params and models, DSAnimStudio animation timelines, and DarkScript3 event scripts.

Blocks: a hybrid.

  • Visuals come from our own render pass inside the game’s frame, depth-tested against its depth buffer. It scales to whole chunks and matches Tobyn’s look, but Elden Ring doesn’t light it.
  • Footing comes from a pool of invisible box-shaped vanilla assets, spawned at runtime (example) and placed only near the player, so you can stand on blocks and pillar up. These real assets also break natively, which is how TNT can work.
  • The cap: each map has an unknown limit on how many assets it can hold, and that limit is the main risk.

Mobs.

  • Stand-ins: invisible Elden Ring characters stand in for Minecraft mobs, spawned the way souls-client does it, into an enlarged character set of 320–480 slots. The Minecraft model is drawn at each stand-in’s position.
  • Hostility: the team type sets who fights whom. “Indiscriminate” (29) is hostile to everyone, so Godrick’s soldiers and creepers go at each other. Allied teams (8 and 12) make friendly golems.
  • Event scripts: these can’t create characters, only toggle ones already placed in the map, so spawning has to happen in the DLL.

Damage both ways.

  • Elden Ring → mob: hook the damage module (precedent: EldenWarfare) and forward hits to the Minecraft side.
  • Mob → Elden Ring: spawn a projectile with an attack param. A creeper’s blast is an explosion projectile.

The Minecraft half is the same as in the Night City plan: a real Minecraft server for blocks, redstone, items and mob brains, linked to the game.

IVSteve ↔ soldier

BLACK OPS loadout swap

Models.

  • Steve: an existing Steve mod replaces an armour set.
  • The soldier: no COD soldier exists; the nearest are Master Chief and the DOOM Slayer. So it’s a custom model rigged to the player skeleton and exported with soulstruct-blender.
  • Animation: on the player skeleton, both move like a Tarnished. Minecraft-style movement means animation work, or drawing Steve in our own pass as Tobyn appears to.

First person. erfps2 runs on me3 and supports 1.17.1. It has a crosshair and free aim, and bow aiming zooms, which gives aim-down-sights.

Guns.

  • Firing: crossbow re-skins with animation edits are the proven route. Existing mods already do 30-shot magazines, burst fire and shotgun spread. The Repeating Crossbow is the full-auto base. Projectile params cover pellet count, spread, fire rate and velocity.
  • True hitscan: a raycast in the DLL, then damage or a projectile at the hit point.
  • Magazines, reloads and ammo counter: Elden Ring has none of these, so they live in the DLL and are drawn with hudhook (precedent: a Nightreign-style HUD).

The swap.

  • Look: Transmogrify Armor changes the player’s appearance at runtime, using the same mechanism as Rock Heart and Lamenter’s Mask.
  • Gear: elden-ring-loadout equips the gun or sword.
  • One hotkey: it sets the look, equips the weapon, toggles first person and swaps the HUD.
  • Precedent: Soul Weapon Transformations already proves full form swaps with their own moveset.

VHard limits and riskiest unknowns

  1. No public tool creates Elden Ring collision. Building at scale depends on the pooled stand-in trick and its capacity.
  2. Asset and character caps under load are unknown.
  3. Every DLL is tied to one game build. A patch breaks it until it’s updated.
  4. The game’s AI only sees real characters. Mobs that are only drawn are invisible to enemies, which is why the stand-ins are needed.
  5. The COD and Minecraft assets are other people’s IP. That’s fine on your own machine, not for sharing.

VIWhat Tobyn proved, and what he didn’t

Proved:

  • it runs on retail Elden Ring under CrossOver;
  • a Minecraft HUD;
  • depth-correct blocks and mobs;
  • Elden Ring AI attacking Minecraft mobs;
  • two-way damage between player and boss;
  • guns draining boss HP;
  • a first-person camera and mode toggles.

Not shown:

  • how it’s built;
  • the claimed item and redstone breadth;
  • building collision at scale;
  • whether mob attacks damage Elden Ring enemies;
  • a third-person soldier.
