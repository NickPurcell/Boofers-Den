HoloTable combat demo reel: running order and captions
=====================================================
File: holotable-combat-reel.mp4, 2:48.7, 6.9 MB, H.264 (High), 1280x720, yuv420p, 30 fps, faststart, silent.
Made by clawcius-engineer12 for jpesus's request on Boofers-Den #65.

How it was made
- Recorded on HoloTable's own code from main at c88f3d2 (17 Nov 2026), on a local copy of the table: three
  signed-in browser windows (the GM, Kira's player, Tobin's player), real clicks for everything on screen.
  No sign-in screen is shown. Cuts only remove waiting; nothing is sped up.
- Characters, names and the cantina are made up. Art: the vetted prop packs by Maveritchell (P. Thompson),
  made for Edge of the Empire (SW Tatooine Mos Shuuta, SW Echoes, SW Bendu and Stalitz). Credited at the end.
- Dice were set in advance so the take could be repeated (said on the end card). Every number, rule and line
  of chat is the table's own.
- Where the point depends on walls (cover, an area stopped by a wall) the shot is the GM's screen: players never
  receive walls, so their previews don't show cover. The captions say so.
- Flash check (ITU-R BT.1702 style, flashcheck.py on the encoded file's frames): worst one-second window has 0.0%
  of the screen with more than 3 flashes (limit 25%); 0.3% at 1.5+ flashes/s; at most 1 whole-frame jump a second.

Left out, and why
- Light and darkvision (#119), unseen in the dark (#120), objects that block sight like walls (#116), and the
  senses work (#128-#135): merged to main after this was recorded. A re-cut can add them.
- Second Wind: shown as a chip in the bar, but spending it is bookkeeping only (it doesn't heal), so the reel spends
  a superiority die instead, which the short rest then visibly gives back.
- A creature's own area attack: areas are cast from characters' powers in the combat view, so the area shown is
  Tobin's power, aimed on the GM's screen.
- The pale "lit squares" don't stand out on this sand floor; the captions name what does show (each square's cost).
